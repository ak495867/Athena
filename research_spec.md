# Empirical Verification of the Birthday Paradox Using 10,000 Simulations

## Objectives
- Validate the theoretical probability of at least two people sharing a birthday in a group of 23 individuals.
- Compare theoretical probability with empirical results from 10,000 Monte Carlo simulations.
- Analyze convergence and confidence intervals.

## Methodology
- Assume 365 equally likely birthdays, ignore leap years.
- For each simulation, randomly assign birthdays to 23 individuals.
- Check if at least one pair shares a birthday.
- Repeat for 10,000 simulations.

## Expected Outcome
- Empirical probability should approximate theoretical value (~50.7%).

## Deliverables
- Code for simulation.
- Validation of claims using external sources.
- Written sections: Introduction, Theory, Simulation, Results, Discussion.
